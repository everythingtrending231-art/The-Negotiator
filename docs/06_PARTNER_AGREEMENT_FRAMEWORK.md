# THE NEGOTIATOR — BUSINESS PARTNER AGREEMENT FRAMEWORK
Version: 1.0
Important: This is a commercial framework, not legal advice or a final legal contract.

## 1. Purpose
Establish a reusable framework for agreements between The Negotiator and participating businesses.

## 2. Parties
- The Negotiator entity: TBD
- Business Partner: specific legal entity
- Effective date
- Term
- Renewal

## 3. Scope
Define products/services covered and territories.

## 4. Commercial Terms
Possible structures:
- Preferred rates
- Percentage discount
- Fixed rates
- Bundled services
- Value-added benefits
- Volume tiers
- Commission
- Referral fee
- Success fee

## 5. Negotiation Authority
Specify whether The Negotiator may:
- Request offers
- Counteroffer
- Accept offers within defined parameters
- Request special pricing
- Negotiate case-specific extras

## 6. Confidentiality
Commercial terms, pricing arrangements, contacts, strategy and proprietary information should be protected subject to applicable law.

## 7. Customer Terms
Define who contracts with the customer and who is responsible for:
- Fulfillment
- Refunds
- Warranty
- Service quality
- Taxes
- Cancellations

## 8. Payment
Specify:
- Who collects payment
- When funds move
- Fees
- Commission
- Taxes
- Refund process

## 9. Service Levels
Define response expectations and availability.

## 10. Brand Use
Define permitted use of:
- The Negotiator name
- Partner badge
- Logos
- Promotional material

## 11. Data Protection
Define permissible customer/business data use and security responsibilities.

## 12. Disputes
Establish:
- Internal escalation
- Negotiation
- Mediation/arbitration/litigation as legally appropriate

## 13. Termination
Specify:
- Notice
- Immediate termination causes
- Treatment of active deals
- Confidentiality survival

## 14. Legal Review
Every production agreement must be reviewed by qualified local counsel in the relevant jurisdiction.
