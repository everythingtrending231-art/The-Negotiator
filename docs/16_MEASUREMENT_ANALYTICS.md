# THE NEGOTIATOR — MEASUREMENT & ANALYTICS
Version: 1.0

## 1. North Star
**Successful negotiated customer outcomes.**

A successful outcome should be defined operationally as a completed case where the customer received an acceptable negotiated offer and the transaction/fulfillment path was successfully completed where applicable.

## 2. Customer Metrics
- Requests submitted
- Requests qualified
- Repeat request rate
- Customer acceptance rate
- Customer satisfaction
- Customer retention
- Referral rate

## 3. Negotiation Metrics
- Cases per Negotiator
- Average negotiation duration
- Business response rate
- Offer rate
- Counteroffer rate
- Final acceptance rate
- Average rounds
- Average price improvement
- Additional value secured
- Total customer benefit

## 4. Business Metrics
- Active partners
- Partner response rate
- Partner conversion
- Partner retention
- Partner revenue generated
- Average response time
- Dispute rate

## 5. Financial Metrics
- Revenue per case
- Gross contribution
- CAC
- Negotiator labor cost per case
- Gross margin
- Lifetime value
- Refund rate

## 6. Quality Metrics
- Documentation completeness
- Unauthorized commitment incidents
- Customer complaints
- Business complaints
- Escalations
- Disputes

## 7. Analytics Principle
Never optimize for discount percentage alone.
A deeper discount can be worse if:
- Fulfillment fails
- Customer experience is poor
- Negotiation takes excessive labor
- Business relationship is damaged

Measure the complete outcome.
