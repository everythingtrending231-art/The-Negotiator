# THE NEGOTIATOR — TRUST, RISK & COMPLIANCE
Version: 1.0

## 1. Purpose
Create a trustworthy platform where customers can safely delegate negotiations and businesses can engage professionally.

## 2. Core Risks
- Fraudulent businesses
- Fraudulent customers
- Fake offers
- Misrepresented prices
- Unauthorized commitments
- Payment fraud
- Identity fraud
- Data leakage
- Disputes
- Unfulfilled deals
- Misleading savings claims
- Regulatory violations

## 3. Business Verification
Potential checks:
- Legal identity
- Registration where applicable
- Contact verification
- Address
- Website/social presence
- Reputation
- References
- Payment information
- Industry-specific licensing where applicable

## 4. Customer Protection
- Clear offer terms
- Offer expiry
- Confirmation before commitment
- Transaction records
- Complaint path
- Refund routing
- Dispute process

## 5. Staff Controls
- Role-based access
- Case permissions
- Approval thresholds
- Audit logs
- Confidentiality
- Staff training

## 6. High-Value Transactions
Require additional controls:
- Customer identity verification
- Business verification
- Written offer
- Clear contract
- Management approval
- Payment verification

Thresholds are TBD.

## 7. Privacy
Collect only data needed for:
- Negotiation
- Communication
- Fulfillment
- Payment
- Compliance

Apply applicable privacy law in each market.

## 8. Commercial Confidentiality
Internal partner agreements and commercial terms should be protected through:
- Access controls
- Confidentiality agreements
- Staff policies
- Secure storage
- Audit trails

## 9. Disputes
Process:
1. Intake
2. Evidence collection
3. Review
4. Communication
5. Resolution
6. Escalation
7. Closure

## 10. Legal Review
The final customer terms, partner agreements, payment structures, consumer disclosures, privacy policies and regulatory model must be reviewed by qualified counsel for each launch jurisdiction.
