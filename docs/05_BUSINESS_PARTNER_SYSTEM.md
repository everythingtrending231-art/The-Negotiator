# THE NEGOTIATOR — BUSINESS PARTNER SYSTEM
Version: 1.1

## 1. Purpose
Build and manage a network of businesses willing to engage with The Negotiator and provide commercially attractive offers.

## 2. Partner Categories
Categories are managed as a live, admin-editable taxonomy via the Category & Business CMS (see `22_CATEGORY_AND_BUSINESS_CMS.md`), not a fixed hardcoded list. The set below is an illustrative starting point, not a final or exhaustive list:

- Hotels and accommodation
- Travel and tours
- Transportation
- Car dealerships
- Car rentals
- Electronics
- Appliances
- Furniture
- Clothing
- Restaurants and catering
- Event venues
- Construction
- Home services
- Repairs
- Photography
- Video
- Graphic design
- Marketing
- Professional services
- Education
- Beauty
- Wellness
- Other services

New categories can be added by an Administrator at any time through the CMS, subject to market validation and regulatory review where applicable. Each category may define its own category-specific request fields (see `22_CATEGORY_AND_BUSINESS_CMS.md`, Section 4.2).

## 3. Partner Acquisition
Channels:
- Direct outreach
- Referrals
- Industry associations
- Events
- Social media
- Local business networks
- Website applications
- Strategic partnerships

In addition to the application-based intake above, an Administrator may create a business record directly through the CMS for businesses onboarded manually (phone, email, in-person) — see `22_CATEGORY_AND_BUSINESS_CMS.md`, Section 5.3.

## 4. Partner Qualification
Verify:
- Legal/business identity as appropriate
- Contact information
- Service/product authenticity
- Reputation
- Fulfillment capability
- Pricing integrity
- Responsiveness
- Complaint history where available

## 5. Commercial Agreement Types
- Percentage discount
- Fixed negotiated price
- Volume pricing
- Bundle
- Added value
- Priority service
- Preferred terms
- Seasonal arrangement
- Case-by-case negotiation
- Combination

## 6. Internal Partner Record
Store:
- Business profile
- Assigned category/categories
- Verification status
- Relationship owner
- Agreement type
- Agreement dates
- Terms
- Restrictions
- Negotiation permissions
- Contact persons
- Communication history
- Performance
- Disputes
- Renewal status
- Customer-visible / publish status (see `22_CATEGORY_AND_BUSINESS_CMS.md`, Section 5.1)

## 7. Partner Expectations
Partners should:
- Respond in good faith
- Honor accepted offers
- Keep pricing information accurate
- Notify The Negotiator of material changes
- Fulfill confirmed deals
- Respect customer privacy
- Follow applicable law

## 8. Partner Benefits
- Qualified leads
- New customers
- Controlled promotional pricing
- Relationship-based sales
- Capacity utilization
- Repeat business
- Performance visibility

## 9. Partner Performance
Track:
- Response rate
- Response time
- Offer rate
- Acceptance rate
- Completed deals
- Cancellation rate
- Customer satisfaction
- Disputes
- Average value created

## 10. Partner Lifecycle
Prospect → Qualified → Verified → Negotiation → Approved → Active → Review → Renew / Suspend / Terminate

This lifecycle is managed and status changes are recorded through the admin CMS; see `22_CATEGORY_AND_BUSINESS_CMS.md`, Section 5.2. A business record can exist and progress through early lifecycle stages before it is published (customer-visible).
