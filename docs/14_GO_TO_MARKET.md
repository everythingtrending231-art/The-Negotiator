# THE NEGOTIATOR — GO-TO-MARKET PLAN
Version: 1.0

## 1. Launch Philosophy
Start narrow, prove the service, then expand.

## 2. Initial Market
Choose one launch geography and a limited set of high-negotiability categories.
A Liberia-first pilot is a possible strategy, but the launch market should be formally validated.

## 3. Ideal Early Categories
Prioritize categories where:
- Prices are negotiable
- Ticket values justify human effort
- Businesses have unused capacity
- Customer value is easy to demonstrate
- Business relationships are feasible

Potential:
- Hotels
- Events
- Car rentals
- Electronics
- Furniture
- Professional services
- Photography
- Catering
- Construction/services

## 4. Partner Acquisition
Goal:
Build a credible initial network before broad customer acquisition.

Approach:
- Select target businesses
- Conduct direct outreach
- Explain customer lead value
- Negotiate commercial terms
- Sign partner agreements
- Train partner contacts
- Test cases

## 5. Customer Acquisition
Channels:
- Referral
- Social media
- Content
- Partnerships
- Influencers/creators
- Business referrals
- Community marketing
- Search
- Direct outreach

## 6. Founding Customer Offer
Potential:
> First negotiation free.

Or:
> No fee unless we secure a deal.

This is a testable offer, not a permanent rule.

## 7. Launch Messaging
Primary:
> “Before you pay the asking price, ask The Negotiator.”

Alternative:
> “Can you negotiate this for me?”

## 8. Trust Building
Show:
- Real customer stories
- Verified partner businesses where appropriate
- Transparent outcomes
- Professional Negotiators
- Clear terms
- No exaggerated savings claims

## 9. Launch Metrics
- Requests
- Qualified requests
- Negotiations started
- Offers received
- Deals accepted
- Customer savings/value
- Revenue
- Repeat customers
- Partner retention

## 10. Expansion
Only expand categories after:
- Repeatable negotiation process
- Adequate partner density
- Positive unit economics
- Reliable fulfillment
- Sufficient Negotiator capacity
