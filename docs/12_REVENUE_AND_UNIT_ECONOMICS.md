# THE NEGOTIATOR — REVENUE & UNIT ECONOMICS
Version: 1.0

## 1. Revenue Philosophy
Revenue should align with successful customer outcomes and sustainable business participation.

## 2. Candidate Models

### Success Fee
A business pays when a transaction closes.

### Customer Service Fee
Customer pays for the negotiation service.

### Subscription
Business pays recurring access/participation fee.

### Lead Fee
Business pays for qualified opportunities.

### Hybrid
Combination of subscription, success fee and/or customer fee.

## 3. Unit Economics
Track per negotiation:
- Customer acquisition cost
- Negotiator labor cost
- Communication cost
- Research cost
- Payment processing
- Business acquisition cost allocation
- Revenue
- Gross margin
- Customer savings
- Customer value created
- Deal completion rate

## 4. Core Formula
Gross contribution per completed case =
Revenue from case - direct case costs.

## 5. Operational Constraint
Human negotiation creates a variable labor cost.

Therefore monitor:
- Average minutes per case
- Cases per Negotiator per day
- Revenue per case
- Gross contribution per Negotiator hour

## 6. Pricing Experiments
Test:
- Free customer negotiation
- Small upfront fee
- Success fee
- Premium/high-value service
- Business-funded model
- Hybrid

Do not finalize pricing without customer and business validation.

## 7. Savings Reporting
Never report savings without a defensible reference price.

Distinguish:
- Advertised/public price
- Verified quote
- Negotiated price
- Additional value
- Total customer benefit

## 8. Financial Dashboard
Track:
- Revenue
- Gross margin
- Active cases
- Completed deals
- Average deal value
- Average customer benefit
- Negotiator productivity
- Partner productivity
- CAC
- Repeat rate
- Refund/dispute rate
