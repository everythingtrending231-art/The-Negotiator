# THE NEGOTIATOR — OPERATIONS & ORGANIZATION
Version: 1.1

## 1. Operating Model
The initial service is human-powered.

Core operation:
Customer request → Case creation → Negotiator assignment → Research → Business engagement → Negotiation → Offer → Customer decision → Fulfillment/closure.

## 2. Initial Team Structure
Recommended starting functions:
- Founder/General Manager
- Negotiation Lead
- Negotiators
- Business Partnerships
- Customer Support
- Operations/Admin
- Product/Engineering support

Actual headcount should follow transaction volume.

## 3. Negotiator Role
Responsibilities:
- Manage assigned cases
- Represent customers

- Negotiate within authority
- Document cases

- Reporting
- Process improvement

## 6. Customer Support
Support should handle:
- Account issues
- Request questions
- Offer clarification
- Transaction support
- Complaints
- Refund routing
- **Post-closure record retrieval** — the sole channel for a customer to obtain their record after a case has closed and dashboard/magic-link access has ended (see `03_CUSTOMER_JOURNEY.md`, Section 10.1). This is never self-service.

Negotiation strategy remains with the Negotiator.

### 6.1 SOP — Post-Closure Record Retrieval
1. Customer contacts Support requesting a closed case record (e.g., deal record, receipt, transaction details).
2. Support locates the case and confirms it is in a terminal status (Accepted, Declined, Expired, Cancelled, Closed).
3. Support verifies the requester's identity and ownership of the case — at minimum, matching the requester to the email address on file for that ticket; additional verification at Support's discretion for sensitive or high-value cases.
4. Support obtains the customer's explicit approval to release the record (e.g., a confirmed reply to "can we send this to your email on file").
5. Support re-sends the closure summary/record directly to the verified email address.
6. The request, verification outcome, and record release are logged in the audit trail per `08_PLATFORM_ARCHITECTURE.md`, Section 6.

No new magic link or dashboard access is issued as part of this process — the record is delivered directly, not through re-opened self-service access.

## 7. SOP Requirements
Create SOPs for:
- New request
- Case assignment
- Customer verification
- Business verification
- Negotiation
- Counteroffers
- Customer approval
- Deal acceptance
- Cancellation
- Dispute
- Fraud
- High-value transaction
- Partner onboarding
- Partner termination
- Post-closure record retrieval (see Section 6.1 above)

## 8. Service Levels
Initial SLAs should be validated through pilot data.
Define:
- Initial review time
- First customer update
- Business contact target
- Offer turnaround
- Escalation response

Do not publish fixed promises until operational capacity is proven.

## 9. Quality Assurance
Review:
- Accuracy
- Negotiation professionalism
- Savings/value calculation
- Customer authority
- Documentation
- Business relationship quality
- Outcome

## 10. Training
Negotiators require:
- Negotiation theory
- Customer representation
- Commercial communication
- Ethics
- Documentation
- Conflict management
- Category-specific knowledge
- Platform procedures

Customer Support requires, in addition to standard support training:
- Identity verification practices for post-closure record requests
- Escalation path for ambiguous or disputed ownership claims
