# THE NEGOTIATOR — CUSTOMER TERMS FRAMEWORK
Version: 1.0
Important: Framework only. Obtain legal review before publication.

## 1. Service Description
The Negotiator provides a professional negotiation service and may facilitate communication between customers and businesses.

## 2. No Guaranteed Outcome
Unless expressly stated in a specific legally approved offer:
- Negotiation does not guarantee a discount.
- Negotiation does not guarantee acceptance.
- Negotiation does not guarantee availability.

## 3. Customer Authority
Customer must provide accurate information and must define any limits that The Negotiator must respect.

## 4. Offer
A negotiated offer should state:
- Product/service
- Business
- Price
- Currency
- Conditions
- Expiration
- Fulfillment
- Payment
- Cancellation terms

## 5. Acceptance
Define when an offer becomes binding and who is contractually responsible for fulfillment.

## 6. Fees
Clearly disclose any:
- Customer fee
- Service fee
- Success fee
- Transaction fee

## 7. Business Responsibility
Where applicable, the business remains responsible for:
- Product/service quality
- Fulfillment
- Warranty
- Refunds
- Legal compliance

## 8. Privacy
Explain data collection, use, storage and sharing.

## 9. Disputes
Provide a clear complaint and dispute process.

## 10. Legal Review
Final terms must be reviewed for each launch jurisdiction.
