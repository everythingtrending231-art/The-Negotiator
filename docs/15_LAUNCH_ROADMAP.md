# THE NEGOTIATOR — PRODUCT & BUSINESS ROADMAP
Version: 1.0

## PHASE 0 — Validation
Objectives:
- Validate customer pain
- Interview customers
- Interview businesses
- Test negotiation demand
- Identify best categories
- Test willingness to pay
- Establish first partner relationships

Deliverables:
- Research report
- Category shortlist
- Partner shortlist
- Pilot operating procedure

## PHASE 1 — Concierge MVP
Run the service largely manually.

Customer:
- Simple website/form
- Request submission
- Status updates

Internal:
- Case management
- Negotiator assignment
- Business contact database
- Negotiation records
- Offer management

Objective:
Prove the business model before heavy software investment.

## PHASE 2 — Platform MVP
Build:
- Customer portal
- Negotiator portal
- Business portal
- Admin portal
- Notifications
- Offer system
- Audit system
- Basic analytics

## PHASE 3 — Transaction Infrastructure
Add:
- Payments
- Deal confirmation
- Receipts
- Refund workflow
- Partner settlement
- Transaction reporting

## PHASE 4 — Network Expansion
Add:
- More businesses
- More categories
- More locations
- Partner performance tools
- Referral systems

## PHASE 5 — Integrations
Add APIs and integrations where volume justifies them.

## PHASE 6 — Advanced Automation
Potentially add:
- Research assistance
- Drafting assistance
- Workflow automation
- Analytics
- Intelligent routing

AI-assisted negotiation remains a separate future decision and is not part of the initial model.

## Gate Criteria
Do not advance based solely on time.
Advance when:
- Customers repeatedly use service
- Businesses respond
- Negotiators can manage workload
- Deals close
- Customers perceive value
- Unit economics are viable
