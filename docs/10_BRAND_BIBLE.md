# THE NEGOTIATOR — BRAND BIBLE
Version: 1.0
Status: Foundation

## 1. Brand Name
**The Negotiator**

## 2. Core Positioning
A professional human negotiation service that represents customers and works with businesses to secure better deals.

## 3. Brand Promise
**You ask. We negotiate.**

## 4. Supporting Lines
Potential:
- Leave the negotiation to us.
- Better deals, negotiated for you.
- Don't accept the first offer.
- We negotiate on your behalf.

These are candidate messages, not all mandatory taglines.

## 5. Brand Personality
- Confident
- Professional
- Resourceful
- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Human
- Strategic
- Respectful
- Assertive without being aggressive

## 6. Brand Should NOT Feel
- Cheap
- Desperate
- Aggressive
- Manipulative
- Corporate-bureaucratic
- Deceptive
- Like a coupon spam site

## 7. Brand Archetype
Primary:
**The Advocate / Professional Representative**

Secondary:
**The Strategist**

## 8. Messaging Framework
Problem:
> People often accept prices or terms because negotiating is inconvenient, uncomfortable or unfamiliar.

Solution:
> Give the negotiation to a professional.

Proof:
> The Negotiator builds business relationships and negotiates on behalf of customers.

Outcome:
> Better price, better terms or better overall value.

## 9. Customer Message
> “Tell us what you want. We'll negotiate it for you.”

## 10. Business Message
> “We bring qualified customers who are ready to buy when the right deal is available.”

## 11. Visual Direction
The identity should communicate:
- Trust
- Competence
- Precision
- Human expertise
- Commercial intelligence

Avoid stereotypical aggressive imagery such as shouting, boxing, or adversarial conflict.

## 12. Logo Direction
A professional wordmark and/or symbol should work:
- In digital interfaces
- On partner badges
- On documents
- In mobile app icons
- In social profiles

Final logo requires design exploration.

## 13. Color System
Do not lock final colors until visual exploration and accessibility testing.
Suggested direction:
- Deep professional base
- High-contrast neutral
- One confident accent
- Functional success/warning/error colors

## 14. Typography
Use a modern, highly legible sans-serif system.
Final font selection requires brand exploration and licensing review.

## 15. Voice
Short, clear, direct and confident.

Say:
> “We've negotiated an offer for you.”

Avoid:
> “Our advanced proprietary negotiation solution has successfully optimized your commercial outcome.”

## 16. Trust Language
Use precise claims.
Never claim:
- Guaranteed savings
- Guaranteed lowest price
- Guaranteed acceptance
unless legally and operationally substantiated.

## 17. Brand Experience
The customer should feel:
> “Someone capable is handling this for me.”
