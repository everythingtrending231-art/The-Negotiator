# THE NEGOTIATOR — CONTENT & MARKETING FRAMEWORK
Version: 1.0

## 1. Content Objective
Teach people that they can delegate negotiation to a professional.

## 2. Core Message
> You don't have to negotiate it yourself.

## 3. Content Pillars

### Negotiation Education
- Why businesses negotiate
- When to negotiate
- How to identify flexibility
- Price vs value

### Customer Stories
- Request
- Negotiation
- Outcome
- Customer benefit

### Business Stories
- How partner businesses benefit
- Customer acquisition
- Capacity utilization

### Product Demonstrations
- Submit a request
- Track a Negotiator
- Review an offer

### Trust
- How we protect customers
- How businesses are verified
- What Negotiators do

## 4. Campaign Concepts
### “Can You Negotiate This?”
Show everyday items/services and invite people to imagine delegating the negotiation.

### “Before You Pay”
Educational content encouraging customers to consider whether a deal can be improved.

### “Leave It With Us”
Human stories showing the Negotiator taking over.

## 5. Tone
Confident, practical, professional and human.

## 6. Avoid
- Fake savings
- Fake scarcity
- Fake competitor offers
- “Guaranteed lowest price” claims without evidence
- Aggressive business-bashing

## 7. Social Content
Potential formats:
- Short negotiation stories
- Before/after outcomes
- Customer testimonials
- Negotiator tips
- Business partner features
- Case studies
