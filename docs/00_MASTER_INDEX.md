Version: 1.1

Date: 2026-08-31

## Purpose
This folder is the foundational documentation set for building The Negotiator as a human-powered professional negotiation platform.

## Core Definition
The Negotiator represents customers in negotiations with participating businesses to secure better prices, better terms, or greater overall value.

## Core Customer Promise
**You ask. We negotiate.**

Primary customer request:
> **“Can you negotiate this for me?”**

## Critical Operating Principle
The customer does not need to know how a deal is obtained. Existing business agreements, preferred rates, commercial terms, partner relationships, and negotiation intelligence are internal assets of The Negotiator unless explicitly disclosed.

## Human-First Constraint
The initial platform is **human-operated**. AI-assisted professional negotiation is explicitly out of scope for the initial operating model. Automation may be considered later, after the human process is proven.
